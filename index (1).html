About

Tegan Parrish is an aspiring writer of fiction and poetry, who seeks to share evocative storytelling and deep engagement with the human condition, the natural world, and the complexities of existence.

Through studying creative writing and political communication, Tegan blends knowledge with creativity to produce compelling narratives. She is passionate about teaching others, and is a proud supporter of literacy initiatives.
